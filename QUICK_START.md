## 📲 Add to Home Screen (iOS Safari)

1. Open the URL in Safari
2. Tap the **Share button** (□ with ↑)
3. Scroll and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**
5. Done! App icon appears on your home screen 🎉

## 📲 Add to Home Screen (Android)

1. Open the URL in Chrome
2. Tap **⋮** (three dots menu)
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**
5. Done! App installed as PWA 🎉

## ⚙️ Enable GitHub Pages (One-time setup)

1. Go to: https://github.com/mohdmansoorak/Health-App/settings/pages
2. Under "Source", select **"Deploy from a branch"**
3. Select branch: **gh-pages**
4. Select folder: **/ (root)**
5. Click **Save**
6. Wait 2-3 minutes for deployment
7. Your app will be live at: https://mohdmansoorak.github.io/Health-App/
